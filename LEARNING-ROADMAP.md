# Integrated Technology, Business, Marketing & Trading Mastery Roadmap

## Mission
Develop employable technical skills, build and operate Micro-SaaS products, manage and optimise digital marketing, and understand financial markets and systematic trading.

This is a multi-stage curriculum. Completion is demonstrated through working projects, practical assessments, and real operating experience—not course certificates alone.

## Curriculum domains

### 1. Software engineering
- Python: programming, automation, data processing, APIs
- JavaScript: language fundamentals, asynchronous programming, browser APIs
- Node.js: backend services, REST APIs, authentication
- React.js: components, state, routing, forms, frontend architecture
- SQL: relational modelling, joins, aggregations, window functions, query optimisation
- Git: branching, merging, conflict resolution, version control
- GitHub: repositories, issues, pull requests, Actions, CI/CD

### 2. Cloud and infrastructure
- Microsoft Azure: compute, storage, networking, databases, identity, monitoring
- Deployment, environment configuration, secrets management, backups
- Application security, access control, reliability and cost management

### 3. Data analytics and business intelligence
- Excel: formulas, tables, PivotTables, Power Query, data modelling and automation
- Power BI: Power Query, data modelling, DAX, reports and dashboards
- Tableau: data preparation, calculated fields, dashboards and storytelling
- Python and SQL for data cleaning, analysis and repeatable reporting
- Statistics, KPI design, data quality and communicating business findings

### 4. Personal knowledge and productivity systems
- Notion: databases, relations, rollups, templates, project and life management
- Obsidian: Markdown, linking, graph-based notes, knowledge capture and retrieval
- Google Calendar: time blocking, recurring schedules, calendar hygiene
- ChatGPT: research, reasoning, writing, data analysis, coding assistance, custom workflows and responsible AI use

### 5. Micro-SaaS product development and entrepreneurship
- Customer discovery, problem validation, competitor research and willingness to pay
- Product strategy, MVP scope, roadmaps, UX and onboarding
- Full-stack architecture using React, Node.js/Python and SQL
- Authentication, authorisation, subscriptions, billing and payment integration
- Azure deployment, observability, security, backups and incident response
- Pricing, MRR, ARR, churn, retention, CAC, LTV and unit economics
- B2B positioning, distribution, customer support and product iteration

### 6. Social media management and digital marketing
- Strategy, positioning, audience research and brand voice
- Content calendars, copywriting, creative production and community management
- Instagram, Facebook, LinkedIn, YouTube and other relevant platforms
- Meta Business Suite and social scheduling/management tools
- Canva and video editing workflows
- SEO, keyword research, Google Analytics 4 and Search Console
- Paid advertising, campaign structure, conversion tracking and attribution
- Marketing dashboards, engagement, reach, leads, CAC and ROI
- AI-assisted content workflows, with human review and platform-policy compliance

### 7. Financial markets, trading and AI-assisted quantitative research
- Market structure, equities, indices, ETFs, bonds, currencies and derivatives
- Investing versus trading; market, limit and stop orders
- Technical analysis, fundamental analysis and options concepts
- Probability, statistics, expected value, position sizing and drawdown
- Risk management, portfolio construction and trading journals
- Trading platforms and research tools: TradingView, broker terminals, options analytics, screeners and market-data services
- Python, pandas, NumPy, Jupyter and SQL for market-data analysis
- Strategy specification, backtesting, out-of-sample testing and paper trading
- Algorithmic execution concepts, APIs, monitoring and failure controls
- AI/ML applications, data leakage, overfitting, transaction costs and model validation

AI outputs and trading signals are research inputs, not guarantees of performance. No live automated execution without extensive testing, explicit risk limits and operational safeguards.

## Recommended learning sequence

1. **Digital foundations:** computer/file skills, Google Calendar, ChatGPT, Notion, Obsidian, Git and GitHub.
2. **Core foundations:** Excel, Python fundamentals, JavaScript fundamentals, SQL and basic statistics.
3. **Analytics employability track:** advanced Excel, SQL, Power BI, Tableau, Python analytics, portfolio projects and interview preparation.
4. **Web engineering track:** JavaScript depth, React, Node.js, APIs, databases, testing and GitHub workflows.
5. **Cloud track:** Azure fundamentals, deployment, networking, identity, security, monitoring and cost controls.
6. **Micro-SaaS track:** customer discovery, MVP, full-stack build, payments, launch, acquisition, retention and unit economics.
7. **Marketing track:** social strategy, content production, SEO, analytics, paid campaigns and optimisation.
8. **Trading track:** market foundations, risk, charting, quantitative methods, Python research, backtesting and paper trading.

The analytics track can be pursued first for an initial employment pathway while software engineering and the other tracks continue progressively. Trading should remain a separate education and research track, not a presumed income source.

## Portfolio projects

- Manufacturing operations dashboard: Excel, SQL, Power BI
- Business performance analysis: Python, SQL, Tableau
- Automated reporting pipeline: Python, SQL, Excel
- Inventory/order management application: React, Node.js, SQL
- Cloud-hosted full-stack application: React, Node.js, Azure
- Micro-SaaS MVP: validated customer problem, subscription workflow and operating metrics
- Social media growth case study: content calendar, channel analytics and optimisation report
- Marketing performance dashboard: GA4/Search Console/ad data and Power BI or Tableau
- Trading research notebook: documented hypothesis, clean data, backtest, costs, risk metrics and paper-trading results
- Personal operating system: Notion, Obsidian and Google Calendar

## Evidence of mastery

For every major skill:
- Learn concepts and terminology.
- Complete guided exercises.
- Practise independently without step-by-step instructions.
- Build a realistic project.
- Document decisions, assumptions and limitations.
- Publish appropriate work to GitHub or a portfolio.
- Test, review and improve the result.
- Explain the work and its trade-offs clearly.

## Progress tracking

Track each skill as:
1. Not started
2. Learning fundamentals
3. Practising with guidance
4. Building independently
5. Portfolio-ready
6. Professional proficiency
7. Advanced / specialist

Do not label a skill expert until independent work, troubleshooting and sustained practical use demonstrate that level.
